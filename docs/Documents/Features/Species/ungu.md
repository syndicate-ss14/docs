## Developer notes

Ungu are a species of large, beefy ungulates. Theyre characterised by their large silhouette and brazen combat.

Ungu are slow, but are capable of speeding themselves up by entering combat mode, although while in this state they take damage from running into walls and other structures, and also have a charge ability that functions similarly to vulpkanin's leap. They also are unable to wear hardsuits, and are limited to "softsuits" like EVA suits.

You might want to enable them if you want variety in melee combat roles, or if you just think theyre cute.

Be advised, however, that they tend to need custom sprites due to their deviation from the standard silhouette, so that may cause your devs extra work down the line.
