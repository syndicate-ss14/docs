## Developer notes

Ovinia are a species of ovine aliens that particularly look like terran sheep and ovines.

They resist heat, cold, and shock, as well as having a reduced flammibility due to the properties of their wool.

In return however, they are weaker to slash and pierce and cannot consume theobromine or allicin.

Additionally, Ovinia's hooved feet protect them from sharp floor hazards like shards of glass.

Most animals additionally are neutral towards Ovinia until provoked due to their docile temperments.

Due to their unique biology and multiple stomachs, ovinia absorb nutrients more efficiently, leading to them to go a longer time without getting hungry or thirsty, though they spend extra time eating and drinking.

You might want to enable them for another morphotype for players to use to make characters, or you might also want to enable them to allow a more pacifist approach to most vent critters. You also want to do so because they're sheepie.
