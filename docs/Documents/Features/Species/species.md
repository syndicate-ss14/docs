# Species

Macrocosm hosts a variety of species that are available for use on downstreams. These species are **disabled by default** and will require manual enabling to appear in-game.
This process is being moved to partial prototypes with PR #64, and this page will be updated with instructions when that has passed.


## Species Categorization

Species in this document are categorised into **two categories**, `Normal` and `Wildcard`

`Normal` species are as you would expect a regular, vanilla space station roundstart species to function. In Base SS14, this would include Moths, Reptiles, Humans, etc.

`Wildcard` species are intended for non-roundstart purposes. You can enable them roundstart if you like, but they either aren't designed or balanced around it, or are missing customisation options your players might usually expect. In Base SS14 this would include Skeletons, Gingerbread Men, etc.

Wildcard species will be denoted with a warning at the beginning of the article giving context, like so:
:::warning
This species is a **wildcard** and is not intended to be made available roundstart.
:::
